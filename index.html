01About the artist

A multimedia artist and producer dedicated to the space where art, technology, and cultural storytelling meet.

Doris (Leping) Cao is a Boston-based filmmaker, photographer, and multimedia artist whose work probes the nuanced undercurrents of gender equality and diversity within everyday life. She holds a Bachelor’s degree from Emerson College’s School of Film, with a background in international film and art.

Doris seamlessly bridges tactile 16mm analog filmmaking and traditional darkroom processes with digital media and spatial design. For her, the photographic frame is never a final destination but a point of departure: her installations extend visual media, liberating moving and still imagery from the constraints of the frame to cultivate experiential environments where audience and narrative converge.

As a filmmaker, Doris is deeply interested in spectatorship and this awareness guides her approach to storytelling. For her, filmmaking is a bridge between individuals and communities; her goal as a director is to amplify diverse voices and create stories that resonate across boundaries. At the core of her artistic practice are questions of visibility, power, and emotional inheritance within contemporary society. She is particularly drawn to stories centered on marginalized and underrepresented voices, especially focus on feminism and the complexities of human connection in modern life.
